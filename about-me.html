About me

Hi, I'm Hassam — a VFX texture artist.

I'm a self-motivated 3D Character & Creature Artist specializing in photorealistic and stylized materials, texture painting and sculpting.

Currently a Senior Texture Painter at Digital Domain Vancouver, I've had the privilege of contributing to prominent titles at Sony and Marvel. My passion is bringing imaginary worlds to life through meticulous detail and a deep understanding of both the technical and artistic sides of visual effects.
